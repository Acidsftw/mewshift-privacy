MewShift ist kostenlos und finanziert sich durch Werbung. Dafür nutzen wir Google AdMob der Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland („Google“). Werbung erscheint gelegentlich zwischen zwei Läufen (nie während eines Laufs) sowie freiwillig, wenn du dir ein Video ansiehst, um eine zweite Chance, ein Gratis-Geschenk oder doppelte Diamanten zu erhalten.

Dabei verarbeitet Google insbesondere folgende Daten:

  • Werbe-ID deines Geräts (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP-Adresse und daraus abgeleiteter ungefährer Standort

  • Interaktionen mit Anzeigen (z. B. angezeigt, angeklickt, Video angesehen)

Rechtsgrundlage ist deine Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO, § 25 Abs. 1 TDDDG). Ohne Einwilligung werden nur eingeschränkte, nicht personalisierte Anzeigen ausgeliefert, soweit dies rechtlich zulässig ist.

Widerruf: Du kannst deine Auswahl jederzeit im Spiel unter Optionen → Mehr → Datenschutz-Optionen ändern oder widerrufen. Zusätzlich kannst du die Werbe-ID in den Android-Einstellungen zurücksetzen oder löschen.

Privacy Policy – MewShift

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the game MewShift (Android) by Acids Studio.

1. Controller

Acids Studio, Jan Saurer
Email: acidsstudio@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (best height and score, diamonds, unlocked cats, accessories and trails, unopened gifts, achievements, daily gift streak, statistics), the status of your purchases, simple counters that keep ads from appearing too often, and your settings (volume, language, vibration, accessibility) are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

MewShift is free and funded by ads. We use Google AdMob by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Ads appear occasionally between two runs (never during a run), and optionally when you choose to watch a video for a second chance, a free gift or double diamonds.

Google processes in particular:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, operating system, language, app version)
  • interactions with ads (e.g. shown, clicked, video watched)
  • diagnostic data for error and fraud detection

Consent: Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked on first launch, through a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform, certified under the IAB TCF), whether data may be used for personalised ads and for accessing information on the device. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, only limited, non-personalised ads are served where legally permitted.

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → More → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

International transfers: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR); standard contractual clauses apply additionally.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. In-game purchases

Optional purchases (starter pack, "No more ad breaks" and diamond packs) are handled entirely by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). You enter payment details only with Google; we never receive them. The app only learns from Google Play which product was bought for your Google account, plus a purchase token so that every purchase is credited exactly once. Both are stored only on your device. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google's privacy policy applies.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads and for purchases. The game itself also works offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) – for ads, see section 3.
  • Vibrate – optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in the settings.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) – only for optional purchases, see section 4.
  • Technical permissions of the ads SDK (e.g. background tasks, wake lock, basic phone state) – so Google can load ads reliably. No access to your phone number, calls, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or GPS location.

6. Children

MewShift is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ads are limited to content that Google rates "PG" (general audiences with parental guidance) or lower.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As we do not store any data about you ourselves, please also contact Google regarding data processed by Google.

8. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here always applies.
